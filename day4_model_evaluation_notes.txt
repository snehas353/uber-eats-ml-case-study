
DAY 4 — ML MODEL EVALUATION

1. Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
Measures the average absolute difference between actual
and predicted delivery times. Lower is better.

2. Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)
Penalizes larger prediction errors more strongly.
Lower is better.

3. R-squared (R2)
Measures how much variation in delivery time is explained
by the model. Higher is generally better.

4. Model Comparison
Linear Regression will be compared with Random Forest
using the same training and testing data.

Note: Model scores will be recorded after training with
a valid delivery-time target column.
