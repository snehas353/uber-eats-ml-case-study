# Uber Eats ML Capstone Project – Day 3

## 1. Objective
The objective of Day 3 is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Uber Eats datasets to understand order patterns, customer behavior, restaurant performance, payment trends, and factors affecting delivery time.

## 2. Dataset Preparation
Used the datasets generated and validated during the earlier stages of the project.

The analysis covered:
- Customers
- Restaurants
- Orders
- Deliveries
- Drivers
- Reviews
- Payments

Reviewed the relevant columns and data types before performing aggregations and visualizations.

## 3. Order Analysis
Analyzed order information to understand order volume and purchasing patterns.

Activities included:
- Examining the distribution of order statuses.
- Analyzing order amounts and their distribution.
- Comparing order counts across cities.
- Identifying hourly ordering patterns.
- Examining daily order trends.

These analyses help identify busy periods, popular locations, and variations in customer ordering activity.

## 4. Delivery Performance Analysis
Investigated delivery-related information to understand operational performance.

Activities included:
- Analyzing delivery time distributions.
- Examining food preparation time.
- Studying delivery distance.
- Comparing delivery performance across different order statuses.
- Identifying patterns that may contribute to longer delivery times.

This analysis provides an initial understanding of delivery efficiency and potential operational bottlenecks.

## 5. Weather and Traffic Analysis
Explored the relationship between external conditions and delivery performance.

Activities included:
- Comparing average delivery times across weather conditions.
- Examining delivery times under different traffic conditions.
- Identifying weather and traffic categories associated with longer delivery durations.

These comparisons help identify possible factors influencing delivery time. The observed relationships do not necessarily establish causation.

## 6. Restaurant Performance Analysis
Analyzed restaurant-level information to understand differences in performance.

Activities included:
- Comparing order volumes across restaurant categories.
- Examining restaurant ratings.
- Analyzing order amounts by category.
- Comparing food preparation times across restaurant categories.

This analysis helps identify differences in customer demand and restaurant operational performance.

## 7. Customer Behavior Analysis
Examined customer activity and purchasing behavior.

Activities included:
- Identifying frequently ordering customers.
- Analyzing repeat-customer activity.
- Examining customer spending patterns.
- Comparing customer activity across cities.

These insights help understand customer engagement, ordering frequency, and purchasing patterns.

## 8. Payment Analysis
Analyzed payment records to understand transaction patterns.

Activities included:
- Examining the distribution of payment methods.
- Comparing successful and unsuccessful transactions.
- Calculating payment success rates where transaction status data was available.
- Creating visualizations to compare payment trends.

This analysis provides an overview of payment behavior and transaction outcomes.

## 9. Data Visualization
Used Matplotlib and Seaborn to create visualizations that communicate findings clearly.

Visualizations included:
- Bar charts for order counts and categorical comparisons.
- Distribution plots for order amounts and delivery times.
- Time-based charts for hourly and daily ordering patterns.
- Comparison charts for weather, traffic, restaurant categories, and payment outcomes.

Visualizations make it easier to identify trends, differences, and potential areas for further investigation.

## 10. Tools and Technologies

## 11. Deliverables
- Exploratory analysis of order and delivery data.
- Customer behavior and restaurant performance analysis.
- Weather and traffic comparisons.
- Payment method and transaction analysis.
- Visualizations illustrating key patterns and trends.
- Documented EDA observations for subsequent machine learning work.

## 12. Outcome
Day 3 focused on understanding the Uber Eats business data through exploratory analysis and visualization. The analysis identified patterns in ordering behavior, customer activity, restaurant performance, payment outcomes, and delivery conditions.

These findings provide a foundation for selecting relevant features and developing machine learning models to predict delivery time and support operational decision-making.
